How to play

  • Move across the scene to stir the wind. Leaves lift and the branches lean.
  • Click a bird perched in the canopy to hear it call back.
  • Click the stream for a ripple and a drop of water.
  • Click the trees or sky to send a gust through the grove.
  • Mix the layers: switch wind, water or birds off and watch that part of the scene go still.
  • Drag the sound map to jump anywhere. The scene follows the sound, not the other way round.
  • Keys: Space play/pause, M mute, arrow keys skip 5 seconds, F fullscreen.
